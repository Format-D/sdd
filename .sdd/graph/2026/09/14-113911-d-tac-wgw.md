SDD will complete the session-log publication design in 20260914-001550-d-tac-n47 under the event-sourced architecture of 20260914-113737-d-cpt-qst, preserving the attachment, publication-ordering, summary-conflict and session-collection requirements below. The publication directive remains the implementation anchor for recorded intent, publication identity, preflight, retry and cancellation.

Reconstruct accepted input, preflight state, staged filename mappings and operation outcomes from the serialized session events. Do not maintain separate authoritative copies of information those events provide. Informational metadata and caches are derived projections. The session persistence boundary accepts only one of competing appends against the same event position; a rejected intent append dispatches nothing and must not automatically resubmit stale events.

Replay reconstructs pending, completed and canceled operation positions without invoking their mutations. An explicit continuation may retry a pending mutation through generic bounded retry handling without resending accepted input. If publication succeeded before its outcome was recorded, the retry recognizes the existing publication and performs only required completion work still outstanding. Once the bounded attempts end, control returns to the agent; reconstruction, reconnection and collection do not initiate further recovery. Cancellation records its outcome and return position, stops further attempts and reports remaining effects. Rollback is a separate possible future capability, with none required in this delivery.

Remove the duplicate attachment-page, staged-blob and materialization digests and size revalidation specified in 20260913-233819-d-tac-45y. Storage must preserve the bytes bound to each immutable blob ID, with conformance tests exercising that guarantee. The mutation digest retained by that predecessor is withdrawn under the publication-identity design.

Publication must not expose an entry before its attachments exist, or remove a WIP marker before its closing evidence exists. Fresh validation still checks graph validity and reference resolution; unrelated additions require no whole-graph revision equality. A summary replacement must condition its write on the document it replaces. A conflicting replacement returns the current summary and the document precondition needed for a deliberate next attempt. Automatic retry must not overwrite the competing replacement. This document-level protection is distinct from the removed mutation-payload digest.

Keep ended sessions readable for the configured retention window decided in 20260730-093011-d-tac-k4q. Collection must leave unended sessions, sessions within that window, unreadable sessions and resources still claimed by pending writes untouched. Free collectible session resources before deleting the session. Collection may run opportunistically or on an operator's schedule; it does not complete pending user writes. The retry and cancellation behavior in 20260914-001550-d-tac-n47 determines their outcomes.

This supersedes 20260913-233819-d-tac-45y and 20260729-082254-d-cpt-4tx. The former's integrity simplification survives here while its retained mutation digest does not. The latter's rebuild was delivered, as recorded in 20260811-234201-s-tac-dq7. Its immediate-deletion policy was already replaced by the retention window, and its pending-write sweep is withdrawn by the current publication decision. Local read-in-place storage and explicit target selection remain; the completed relocation removal is not another implementation task. Older pending-intent formats require the explicit compatibility decision called for in 20260914-001550-d-tac-n47.

Verify the existing capture-with-attachment, interrupted-publication and same-entry retry requirements through local and external compositions. Include competing appends at one event position, replay without mutation dispatch, publication succeeding before outcome recording, bounded retry returning control, cancellation with remaining effects, concurrent summary replacement and collection with pending writes. Reconstruct the tested session state from its events without supplementary authoritative session records.
