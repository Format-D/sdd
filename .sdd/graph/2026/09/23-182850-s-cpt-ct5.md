The project event stream of 20260906-113911-s-cpt-ikx and WIP markers are the same thinking: both track movement in the outside world that the graph does not record but sdd should know about, and the stream is the general form of what a marker does for the single case of claimed work. Reading the marker work of 2026-09-23 against that idea sharpens the earlier insight in four places.

Where the stream lives, and who sees it, is a composition choice behind one interface. The local composition keeps a stream on the machine that is not shared: not because local projects lack shared work, they are often shared, but because there is no easy way to share the stream locally, and Git, the one channel local sdd shares through, has proven the wrong tool for it, as the WIP markers showed. Other compositions can choose a shared implementation, and a provider offering a shared stream could later be plugged into local sdd as well. Procedures are the same in every case. A simple, even naive, local stream is enough to start.

Agents and tools publish events directly, without the recorded-intent machinery that graph mutations carry. An event is an occurrence noted, not a mutation whose delivery must be proven: a lost event costs one observation and never consistency, so the intent, retry and cancellation positions built for captures would be weight without a purpose here.

Events are pure signals without reasoning: what happened, when, by whom, about what. Interpretation, synthesis and durable capture belong to consumers reading the stream against the graph, where 20260906-113911-s-cpt-ikx already places them; nothing in the stream argues.

The name settles as "project event stream".

What stays open is the modelling: who defines the event shapes, and how projections are exposed, the in-flight view that would replace markers among them. Until that is decided, 20260923-121943-d-tac-lqh keeps markers as the interim carrier of work movement. This entry records the understanding and commits to nothing; the decision that builds the stream follows the modelling.
