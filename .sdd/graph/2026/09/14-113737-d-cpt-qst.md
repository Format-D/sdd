SDD uses an event-sourced session model: the serialized session event log is the authoritative source of session state, and mutations triggered by engine operations must support idempotent retry. Information reconstructible from session events must not be maintained independently elsewhere. Derived projections and caches may improve access, but remain disposable and reconstructible from the events.

The session persistence boundary enforces one serialized event order. Competing appends against the same position cannot both succeed, and a rejected intent append cannot dispatch its operation. Replay reconstructs state; reconstruction alone never triggers pending operations or recovery work.

The engine executes operations, which may invoke mutations. Mutation failure is an expected outcome. Retrying preserves accepted intent and destination and must not duplicate publication or silently overwrite competing work. Event ordering and mutation idempotency protect different boundaries: a mutation can succeed before its outcome is recorded, so another attempt must recognize effects already applied. Success means all required durable effects are complete.

Generic retry handling makes bounded automatic attempts and returns control promptly enough for the agent to continue the dialogue, following 20260829-113416-d-cpt-nut. Recovery beyond those attempts requires explicit continuation. Cancellation stops further attempts and reports remaining effects; it does not imply rollback. Rollback may become a separately supported mutation capability, but is not required by this decision. Background recovery is never automatic.

SDD owns workflow and graph semantics through the shared application described in 20260830-114446-d-cpt-xc3. Compositions supply infrastructure and authorization policy. Public boundaries follow the subsystems a composition owns, each enforcing its guarantees at its authoritative boundary. Consumers rely on those guarantees instead of duplicating the owner's integrity checks. Shared conformance tests and end-to-end exercises establish equivalent behavior across local and external compositions.

The session event log's authority is bounded by the session's lifetime, following 20260727-224047-d-cpt-u8o. Reconstructible session state protects continuity during a dialogue; the graph remains the project's durable record. Incomplete bookkeeping must not become misleading recovery work or an obligation to preserve sessions indefinitely.

This supersedes 20260730-171311-d-cpt-0cv and 20260712-194427-d-tac-wjq. Their enduring concerns are reliable publication and clear ownership, but their mechanism prescriptions no longer form the current design. Session-log intent and publication identity in 20260914-001550-d-tac-n47 replace frozen payloads, mutation digests and background completion. The delivery recorded in 20260719-191802-s-tac-4bx removed exclusive holders. The predecessors' port inventories, versioning prescriptions, whole-graph revision checks and recovery machinery cease to govern future design as a package. Concrete publication conditions, resource lifecycle and retry behavior belong in implementation decisions; exported signatures, event formats and transaction mechanisms must justify how they preserve the guarantees above.
