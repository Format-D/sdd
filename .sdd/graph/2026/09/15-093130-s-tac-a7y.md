A capture whose draft fails the mechanical write validation after its intent is recorded leaves the session gated until it is cancelled. Found while reviewing the delivery recorded in 20260915-085829-s-tac-awi, by reading `CreateEntry` at merge `547ef763`: ref, closes and supersedes resolution and the type-system write rules run inside the mutation, after the engine appended the intent event and before any LLM call. Pre-flight ran the same checks and passed, so what reaches this point is the graph moving between pre-flight and confirm, such as a ref target superseded meanwhile; that fails identically on every retry, and the pending intent blocks every instance until `next` with the cancel reference. Expected, stated by Christopher in this review: the mechanical validation runs before the intent is recorded, and the write operation does not re-decide it against a later graph state, so an intent that was valid when recorded publishes.
