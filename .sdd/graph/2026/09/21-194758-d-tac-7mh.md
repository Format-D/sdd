Retry and cancellation know only an operation's intent, the command and its recorded values; what the mutation did is the operation's knowledge, reported through a declared effects report that the engine passes through unread.

The generic layers stay ignorant of the mutation so that one runs through any connector: a Git-backed store, a hosted publication store, or a system that is not a graph (20260915-090615-d-tac-ugt). A command may declare an effects report, a function given the recorded intent that returns effects, each a kind, an identifier and a state named by the operation. The serve carries them as fields and one generic line of prose; a command without a report leaves only its recorded values. Effects are read from storage at serve time, never recorded (20260914-113737-d-cpt-qst). The capture command reports its entry as published or absent, and as unknown when its lookup fails.

This places the entry name 20260921-185706-d-tac-t6u requires on the cancellation serve, and gives the remaining-effects report of 20260914-001550-d-tac-n47 its owner. Cleanup is a separate decision.
