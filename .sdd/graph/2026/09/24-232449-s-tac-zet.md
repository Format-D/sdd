Once an implementation run has recorded its closing done, the procedure does not put the merge to the user, and it serves landing text to runs that have nothing to land.

Expected: branch and worktree runs end with the merge as the user's decision (20260923-185316-d-cpt-5gk); the user says when, often after CI or review rounds, and the agent then merges keeping the commits (20260924-231829-d-cpt-r1c). InPlace and quick runs get no landing text.

Observed in 20260924-120119-s-tac-h61: the worktree run skipped the merge offer and the user had to ask for it; the inPlace and quick runs were served landing and binding-clearing text that applied to neither. In the closeout of 20260706-170000-d-prc-imp the merge is one sentence, and the step's only question is evaluate or finish.
