Semantic validation of a graph mutation runs before its durable intent is recorded, and the write operation guards only physical correctness and idempotency. Everything that judges a mutation's content against the graph, resolving refs, closes and supersedes, the type-system write rules, and the pre-flight and writing-guide checks, runs on the graph as it stands when the participants confirm, and the intent records that judgment. That view is good enough: an intent is a decision taken at a point in time, and a graph that moves afterwards does not unmake it. Concurrent captures may land entries that contradict each other; the graph carries contradiction and resolves it through later entries, never by refusing the second write. Under the append-only design a referenced entry does not vanish, so a reference that resolved when the intent was recorded still resolves when it is written.

The write operation therefore checks only what it alone can see: the produced files are structurally readable and complete, attachments exist before the entry is exposed, the publication is not already present under its key, and the composition's completion steps finished. Its failures are mechanical, storage refusing, Git failing, a commit missing, and each is retried or cancelled through the recorded intent. A semantic failure surfacing at write time is a defect in the order of operations, not a condition to handle, because a retry cannot change the input that failed.

In 20260914-113911-d-tac-wgw, "fresh validation still checks graph validity and reference resolution" names that single validation, not a second one at write. The order follows from 20260914-113737-d-cpt-qst, where mutation failure is an expected outcome resolved by bounded retry and explicit continuation, which only holds when the retry cannot fail for a reason it cannot change. It states the expectation behind 20260915-093130-s-tac-a7y, whose fix moves the mechanical validation into the command's prepare step and closes that gap. It takes the step 20260906-234852-d-tac-vdu deferred when it kept write-time revalidation for the acquired-reads delivery: with intent-time validation in place, that revalidation goes, and the write's remaining preconditions are the physical ones above.
